Six ways to open the interview page

The black pill sits in the bottom-right corner of the chooser. Press Open it on any card to see that behaviour for real, with the actual interview page inside. Escape closes.

Its own page 01

The pill is an ordinary link. The chooser is replaced; the browser Back button returns to it.

Simplest. Loses the chooser on screen, keeps a shareable URL.

Slide-over panel 02

A column slides in from the right. The chooser stays visible and dimmed on the left.

Keeps context. Narrow column; the table of items wraps more.

Centred modal 03

A window lifts over the middle of the screen, the page behind it dimmed.

Familiar. Reads as a detour you must close before going on.

Bottom sheet 04

The page rises from the same edge as the pill, stopping short of the top.

The movement explains where it came from. Tall lists scroll inside.

Full-screen overlay 05

The notes take the whole window, with a close button where the pill was.

Most room to read. No visible way back except the close button.

Inline under the header 06

The cards give way and the notes open in place, under the same title.

One screen, one story. The chooser is no longer clickable while open.
